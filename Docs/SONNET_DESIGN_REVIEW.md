# Perfect World: Awakening — Design Review

## 1. Balancing 1000 Points Without Wealth Dominance

The core risk: if "family resources" is one purchasable trait among twenty, players will min-max it because money buys flexibility (better gear, tutoring, time freed from chores) that compounds across every other system. Three concrete mitigations:

- **Split "family" into non-fungible sub-currencies.** Instead of one "Family Resources" trait that buys generic advantage, separate it into things like *Household Stability*, *Financial Cushion*, *Parental Expectations*, and *Community Standing*. Each should carry distinct narrative costs (e.g., high Financial Cushion pairs with high Parental Expectations by default, unless the player spends extra points to decouple them). This prevents a single high-rank purchase from being strictly dominant.
- **Cap total advantage-category spend, not just per-trait rank.** With cumulative cost 0/25/60/110/180 and a max of four rank-4 traits, a player can already dump ~600-700 points into 4 maxed advantage traits. Consider a hard rule: no more than 40% of the 1000-point budget may go into "opportunity/advantage" traits as a category, forcing spend into physical/cognitive/social/talent for real breadth. This is a soft-cap design pattern common in Fallout/Disco Elysium-style systems and keeps wealth from crowding out personality.
- **Make wealth diegetically leaky, not permanent.** If Financial Cushion is high at character creation, tie it to a depletable resource in the Class XI year (school fees, coaching costs, family medical events) rather than a permanent multiplier. This converts a start-of-game stat purchase into an ongoing gameplay resource, which is far more balanceable than a flat bonus.
- **Diminishing returns on stacking wealth + talent.** If a build buys both high Prior Experience and high Family Support, apply a small negative interaction (e.g., "sheltered" narrative flag reducing social trait effectiveness) so the two don't simply multiply.

Given your explicit rule that identity never changes power and buying circumstances mustn't imply worth, the visible fix is to keep power differentials narrow (~10-15% effect size) and let narrative texture, not raw stat advantage, be where family circumstance shows up.

## 2. Age-16 Opening Story Pacing

Recommend a **three-beat first session** structured around a single in-game day-to-week arc, since players need to feel the "life sim" loop before the "Gate" hook lands:

1. **Morning routine + family (10-15 min):** Wake in Nabapara, brief family interaction establishing the household's circumstance (as generated by traits, shown not told), walk to Nabapara Vidyalaya. This is where the character-creation choices become legible — the player *sees* the consequence of their point allocation rather than reading a stat sheet.
2. **School day + social web (15-20 min):** Class XI schedule, 2-3 friendship/rivalry introductions, one skill-check moment tied to an academic or extracurricular trait. This is the system that must "fully work" per your design goals — treat it as the actual first vertical slice, not a tutorial wrapper around the Gate.
3. **The hook (10 min):** End the first day/week with an ambiguous supernatural event near the neighborhood (not yet a Gate encounter) that ties explicitly to a skill the player already exercised in beat 2 (e.g., a chemistry aptitude check reacts to something at the incident site). This satisfies "awakening linked to practiced skills" without front-loading combat before the sim loop is established.

Avoid opening directly on a market/clinic/training tour — that's an interface tour, not a story beat. Fold those locations in as errands across the first in-game week instead of a day-one gauntlet.

## 3. Vertical Slice Limits and Acceptance Criteria

Scope the vertical slice tightly — one district, five locations (school/home/market/clinic/training) plus first Gate — with acceptance criteria that separate "systems prove out" from "content is exhaustive":

**Must include:**
- Character creation with all 20 traits functional and refundable pre-confirm, with save/load of a finished character.
- Home → school → market → clinic → training loop completable across a simulated week with time-of-day and calendar advancement.
- At least 3 scored school interactions (friendship gain/loss, one family obligation conflict, one academic skill check) with visible state persistence.
- One Gate encounter reachable only via the skill-linked hook, with a combat/exploration loop distinct from the life-sim loop, running at the target frame budget on the Android performance gate device.

**Should explicitly NOT be in scope for the slice** (state this to avoid scope creep): additional districts, careers/adulthood systems, national-scale content, iOS/console builds, additional cities beyond Kolkata, romance systems beyond scaffolding.

**Exit criteria:** a first-time player can go from character creation to completing the first Gate in under 90 minutes without a QA facilitator intervening, and the build sustains target frame rate on the lowest supported Android reference device for the whole loop, not just the Gate segment (life-sim scenes with NPC crowds are often the actual mobile bottleneck, not combat).

## 4. Culturally Coherent India World Design

Since Nabapara and its school are fictional, treat this as "a real Kolkata-adjacent neighborhood that happens not to exist," not a generic "Indian town" backdrop:

- **Consult regional specificity, don't generalize.** West Bengal school culture (Class XI stream selection — Science/Commerce/Arts — board exam pressure, Bengali/English medium distinction, local festivals like Durga Puja/Saraswati Puja timing) should shape the calendar and dialogue systems, not just flavor text. Genericizing "India" across Kolkata specifically will read as inauthentic to the exact audience most likely to notice.
- **Family/social systems should reflect real diversity of household structure** (joint family vs. nuclear, income variance, religious plurality within Bengal) without the game moralizing about which is "better" — this reinforces your existing rule that circumstance ≠ worth.
- **Language handling:** "free language" choice at creation should map to actual in-world effects (which dialogue options/NPCs respond differently) rather than being purely cosmetic, or it will feel like a settings toggle instead of an identity choice.
- **Sensitivity review checkpoint:** given the setting is a real, specific, living culture (not fantasy), budget for a cultural consultant or Bengali-speaking reviewer pass before any public showing of the vertical slice, particularly around depicting caste-adjacent, religious, or economic themes through the family-circumstance system.

## 5. C++/Unreal Cross-Platform Pitfalls

- **Android-first performance gate is the right instinct, but validate on real low/mid-tier hardware early**, not emulators or flagship devices — isometric life-sim scenes with many simulated NPCs (school crowds, market stalls) tend to be CPU/tick-bound rather than GPU-bound, which is the opposite bottleneck profile from the Gate combat scenes. Profile both separately.
- **Blueprint vs. C++ split:** for a systems-heavy life sim (traits, relationships, schedules), keep the simulation layer in C++ with data-driven config (data tables/JSON) and reserve Blueprint for UI/scripted sequences — mixed heavy Blueprint logic in simulation loops is a common mobile perf trap in UE.
- **Windows-first dev with Android as the real target** risks late-discovered divergence (input, memory limits, shader compilation stalls, texture streaming). Get a bare-bones Android packaging pipeline running in week one of production, even before content exists, so packaging/perf issues aren't discovered at the end of the slice.
- **Don't build for iOS/console readiness now** — but avoid hard platform-specific dependencies (Windows-only save format, PC-only input assumptions) so the door isn't accidentally closed.

## 6. Next 5 Priorities

1. Lock the trait-cost/soft-cap math for the 1000-point system and playtest 5-10 sample builds specifically hunting for a dominant wealth strategy.
2. Build the Android packaging + profiling pipeline before content production starts.
3. Prototype the day-1 story beat (home→school→hook) as a paper/greybox flow to validate pacing before art investment.
4. Define the vertical-slice exit criteria (above) as a written doc the team signs off on, to prevent scope creep into careers/national content.
5. Schedule a cultural/regional consultation pass on Nabapara/Kolkata-specific content before the slice is shown externally.
