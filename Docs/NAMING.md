# Setting Naming Decision Record

**Status:** design recommendation for preproduction. None of the fictional names below has completed legal, trademark, domain, store-listing, localization, or real-place uniqueness clearance. Production must run those checks before marketing or content lock.

## Recommended launch naming

Use **Kolkata, West Bengal** as the real city and state anchor. Set the opening playable area in the fictional neighborhood **Nabapara**, with the protagonist attending the fictional **Nabapara Vidyalaya**.

This structure gives players an immediately legible region while keeping the street graph, households, school events, Gate damage, clinic cases, businesses, and characters fictional. Copy should say “Nabapara, Kolkata” and describe Nabapara as an invented composite neighborhood. It should not call it a municipal ward, claim that it replaces a real locality, or place a fictional scandal at a real school.

`Nabapara` is a plausible, generic Bengali formation meaning a new neighborhood or quarter. That plausibility is useful, but it also means the name may already occur in real addresses or organizations. The recommendation is therefore a creative direction, not proof of uniqueness.

### Canonical working names

| Use | Working name | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Real launch city | Kolkata | Use real geography selectively and label fictionalized routes clearly. |
| Fictional starting neighborhood | Nabapara | Compact para containing the school, homes, market, bus stand, clinic, and canal works. |
| Fictional school | Nabapara Vidyalaya | Plain, locally legible, and tied to the neighborhood; affiliation and school details remain research-dependent. |
| Display location | Nabapara, Kolkata, West Bengal | Preferred first-use form in maps, save summaries, and production briefs. |

Stable internal IDs should remain descriptive and independent from display-name changes. For example, `region_kolkata`, `district_nabapara`, and `school_nabapara_vidyalaya` can be migrated separately if clearance requires new player-facing names.

## Later real-city candidates

These are expansion candidates, not interchangeable Kolkata districts:

| Real place | Best future use | Production implication |
|---|---|---|
| Howrah | river crossings, rail-linked work, dense residential and industrial histories | Treat as its own city context; do not reduce it to a Kolkata transit backdrop. |
| Durgapur | planned-city form, industry, technical education, and a different Gate-response economy | Requires a distinct environment kit, labor history, and local review. |
| Siliguri | North Bengal transport gateway with different climate, languages, and regional links | Requires a separate regional bible; do not reuse Kolkata weather, speech, or street assumptions. |

Rural Bengal, the Sundarbans, the western plateau districts, and hill regions are separate research tracks. Their landscapes and communities should never be compressed into one “West Bengal” expansion map.

## Fully fictional city alternatives

*Owner decision, 2026-09-25: cities are real (`GLOBAL_WORLD.md` §8), so these alternatives are retired and kept only as a record.*

If production later decides that a real Kolkata anchor creates too many geographic or clearance constraints, evaluate these fictional-city options:

| Proposed name | Strengths | Risks and checks |
|---|---|---|
| Tilottama Nagar | Evokes a poetic epithet associated with Kolkata and supports a beautiful, layered metropolis | Can sound literary or idealized; likely cultural and existing-use associations need review. |
| Nabakolkata | Immediately signals a Kolkata-inspired alternate city and the “new city” premise | May imply replacement or judgment of real Kolkata; can feel derivative and may be difficult to protect. |
| Gangapur | Easy to say across several languages and connects naturally to a river-city identity | Very common place-name pattern with probable real-world overlaps; weakest uniqueness and strongest clearance burden. |

All three are **proposed working options only**. No claim is made that they are unused, legally available, culturally neutral, or suitable in every language.

## Decision rule

Keep the real **Kolkata + fictional Nabapara** approach while the story remains grounded in recognizable Class XI life and one contained district. Reconsider a fully fictional city only if the plot requires large-scale changes to Kolkata's geography, governance, or public history that repeated disclaimers cannot make clear.

Before final approval, test names with Bengali-speaking writers and players, check pronunciation and unintended meanings in launch languages, search real schools and localities, conduct legal clearance, and verify that signage works in Bengali and Latin scripts. These checks may change display names without changing the world structure or stable content IDs.
