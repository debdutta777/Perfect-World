# Phase 3 closure and Phase 4 plan - 15 November 2026

Written by gpt-6-astra (read-only review), recorded by the project head. The three risks in section 1 are v0.9.0 closure work (orders P3-13 and P3-14).

**1. Three risks and decisions**

- **Legacy lives can permanently miss Awakening.** R9-04/P3-08 deliberately leave old saves unseeded; seeded saves already past 18:00 can also lack countdown state. **Decision:** Opus owns explicit compatibility recovery. Preserve authenticated history; reconstruct only supported inputs, never invented lessons or conduct. Before the deadline, offer supervised make-up without extending preparation. For unrecoverable post-deadline saves, preserve the slot and clearly identify legacy compatibility mode, with a new-life option. Never silently strand the player.

- **Homework availability depends on when the catalogue became bound.** P3-06 confirms missed issuance boundaries receive no backfill, undermining absent-student retrieval. **Decision:** reconcile eligible missing assignments once through an authoritative command, using stable IDs. Preserve submissions and existing deadlines; explicitly disclose a recovery window for newly recovered work. Opening the desk must remain read-only. Test repeated reconciliation and reload.

- **Correct clock guards can become permanent player traps.** Open lessons block progression; broken choices therefore block the game. Countdown contacts introduce obligations that ordinary time cannot bypass, while partial skips require explicit handoff to supervision. **Decision:** retain atomic guards and paused reading, but guarantee reachable resume/leave-early, make-up, honour and negotiation actions—including after Continue. Prove zero-practice progression with real contacts bound; disabling contacts is insufficient.

These are **v0.9.0 closure work**, not debts to postpone into Phase 4.
