# Phase 2 plan: Class XI life and the examination, 2026-10-25 (gpt-6-astra)

Proposed for `Docs/PHASE2_PLAN_2026-10-25.md`. Read-only review; no files edited, builds run or agents dispatched.

The player can live from 2 June 2042 through the national virtual-tower examination on **2 February 2043** and into a meaningful following week: follow a Class XI timetable in Kolkata, play at most two foreground lessons daily, maintain friendships, negotiate household responsibilities, study, complete homework and receive diagnostic marks. Shopping, rest, neighbourhood activities and skipping commitments remain legitimate choices. Awakening is compulsory; practice and examination participation are optional. This connects **M2: One school day**, **M3: The Class XI year**, and **M4: Awakening and examination**. It establishes reusable foundations for **M5: Adult life and skeletal later life**, without absorbing M6’s living nation, M7’s world, M8’s breadth or M9’s final art.

**Reuse before expansion**

- `DayLoop` already composes condition, activities, practice limits, attendance and opportunity receipts. World commands include `school.plan_day`, `school.arrive` and `day.apply`.
- `Attendance` supplies stream timetables, lateness and calendar-aware attendance. Content contains three schedules and seven activities.
- `StructureModel/Explain/Investigate/Coordinate` provide case selection, action quotes, outcomes, mastery and routine resolution: eight model cases and four cases per other structure.
- `Assessment` supplies the eighteen-unit, five-group paper and diagnostic marking. `Relationships`, `Threads`, `AptitudeProjection`, `WeekCompression` and `TimeContract` supply reusable rules.
- `Tower` already scores nine routes across three floors, credentials and offers against a synthetic cohort. `Countdown` supplies preparation and obligations; `Awakening` supplies evidence-based profiles and fifteen recipes.
- `Commerce`, `QuestLog`, `LaneJournal` and `NeighbourhoodPlan` support everyday continuity, remembered agreements and canonical destinations.

Missing connections include persistent lesson/mastery ownership, homework, academic records, social consequences, school-aware compression, playable structure panels, tower runners and results-to-life transitions. Standalone APIs are not evidence of integrated gameplay. Treat Round 8/9 branch work as dependencies; audit it before commissioning replacements.
