**3. DESIGN DECISIONS**

- **Academic foundation:** seed bounded academic skill from the creation trait through one explicit table. Label it prior preparation, never observed aptitude or fictional lessons. Actual learning adds evidence; marks do not directly buy power.
- **Attendance versus lessons:** separate attendance-required dates from ordinary-period eligibility. Sports and assessments replace lessons; preparation break creates no absence.
- **Homework issuance:** use authoritative calendar/content triggers, stable assignment IDs and disclosed deadlines. Opening a panel never creates work. Compression processes the same triggers.
- **Growth ownership:** retain R9’s single latent owner. Credit accepted outcomes after practice limits; attribute evidence must not silently become attribute growth.
- **Absence and compression:** interrupt before compulsory supervision is bypassed; require a playable make-up before the fixed deadline. Withdrawal still grants. Countdown opens at the existing 18:00 boundary independently of attendance.
