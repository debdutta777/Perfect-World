# Round 8 plan: everyday commerce, 2026-10-22 (gpt-6-astra)

**Status:** proposed, read-only; no files edited or verification run.

**Completion target:** a sixteen-year-old Class XI student can approach an open, staffed counter, buy an everyday item, inspect the debit and inventory, consume supported food, and recover the exact result after restarting. This delivers Round 6 upgrade #2. Round 7 destinations require actual staffing before sales activate.

## 1. Minimal portable contract

**Existing foundation:** `WorldState` owns `DayLoop`, including condition, activities and opportunity receipts. `LaneJournal` records conversations; `ConductLedger` records tutorial conduct. Neither is a monetary ledger. Resource content distinguishes personal INR from the non-lootable household budget; no wallet transaction system exists. Creation sits beside the world snapshot.

**Money and allowance.** Add `CommerceState` with nonnegative `int64` paise, inventory, allowance cursor, transaction revision and bounded receipts. Initialize through one portable function using validated creation’s Household Resources rank. Persist its funding policy; cross-check against creation on load.

Proposed starting wallet and weekly allowance, respectively:

| Rank | Origin | Start / weekly |
|---|---|---:|
| 0 | Institutional care | ₹120 / ₹120 |
| 1 | Precarious household | ₹150 / ₹150 |
| 2 | Working household | ₹300 / ₹300 |
| 3 | Middle household | ₹600 / ₹600 |
| 4 | Comfortable household | ₹1,200 / ₹1,200 |

Rank 0 receives a children’s-home personal allowance, with equal dignity and purchasing rights. These are discretionary funds; meals, schooling and essential supplies retain baseline access. The **1,000 creation points remain creation points**, never exchangeable currency. Identity and appearance remain free.

Credit every seven game days from initialization; the initial wallet covers the first period. Persist the next due instant. All clock-advancement paths settle crossed boundaries exactly once, including partial advances and future sleep/compression. Checked arithmetic and identical split/bulk results are mandatory.

**Items and shops.** Validated content defines stable item IDs, names, units, stack limits and supported effects. Begin with 12 inventory slots, ten units per stack. Shop content binds catalogue, integer prices, keeper, counter anchor and opening calendar. Availability is the intersection of business hours and service-ready staffing.

**Service and purchase.** Add portable service request/close commands and `commerce.buy(shop,item,quantity,expectedRevision)`. `WorldSim` owns transient service context; it recomputes schedules and `ResolveNpcDecisionAt`, including safe-pause history. Physical approach is explicit bridge input; price and “keeper ready” are never widget authority. Waiting continues game time; open the panel only after the safe pause. Reload invalidates service context.

Revalidate on confirmation: open counter, present keeper, safe pause, awake/free player, settled world, valid item/quantity, capacity, funds and revision. Debit, delivery, revision and receipt commit together. Every refusal preserves state, sequence, log and hash. Duplicate confirmations fail their revision check.

Receipts retain timestamp, shop, item, quantity, actual unit/total price and balances. Keep 64 recent entries plus folded credit/debit totals; folding must preserve reconciliation and duplicate protection.

**Consumption.** `inventory.consume` removes one item atomically and clamps existing `DayLoop.Condition` fields. Tea grants +2,000 Energy, at most once per 60 game minutes; persist the eligibility instant. Foods reduce Hunger by modest authored amounts, 1,000–5,000. No learning bonuses. Plasters and ORS remain carryable supplies with clearly unavailable use until relevant conditions exist; no generic healing.

Reading is paused. Round 8 purchase/consumption commits declare zero minutes; waiting uses the existing clock. Active activities refuse consumption, preventing duplicate condition accounting.

**Persistence.** Current schema/hash are **9/6**; target **10/7**, with `pw.commerce/1`. Hash every authoritative field canonically. Authenticate historical v9/hash-6 and v8/hash-5 using their original encodings before migration, including original door text. Preserve older saves’ honest verification labels. Migration grants the household-derived starting wallet once, empty inventory and a next allowance seven days after the saved instant—no historical backpay. Freeze an untouched v9 fixture and expected migrated result; retain all earlier fixtures.

## 3. Interface

Expose **Buy** at the customer counter alongside Talk/Browse. Show specific unavailable reasons: closed, keeper away, finishing work, insufficient funds or full bag.

A compact paused panel shows item/unit, quantity, unit price, total, wallet and wallet after, using portable quotes. Confirm submits once; success displays the receipt. HUD and pause show wallet; pause Inventory offers supported Consume actions and transaction history.

Keyboard/controller support includes directional navigation, quantity adjustment, confirm/back, reliable focus restoration and readable explanations. Verify both cameras and prevent interaction input from confirming a purchase accidentally.

## 5. Risks and exclusions

Guard allowance farming, duplicate input, stale quotes and tea spam. Test midnight, closing, partial advances and reload boundaries. Bundle persistence changes into one version bump.

Defer resale, stock depletion/restocking, spoilage, debt, banking, household-budget simulation, bargaining, crafting, medical treatment, tailoring orders and a citywide economy. Ordinary purchases should enrich student life before commerce expands.
