# Round 7 plan: a Kolkata neighbourhood, 2026-10-20 (gpt-6-astra)

**Completion target:** walk from home through a populated para to Nabapara Vidyalaya, visit staffed shops, reach a working main road, navigate using both maps, and ring before entering another household. Blocks remain the visual medium through M8; final art remains M9. This is a read-only plan, not implementation or runtime verification.

**Visual priorities, highest impact per effort**

1. **People and destinations:** white/blue uniformed students converging on a clearly signed school gate; shopkeepers behind counters; tea-stall groups; commuters at a bus stop.
2. **Street hierarchy:** intimate lane → para road → main road. Different widths, traffic, frontage rhythms and crossing spaces should make each recognisable.
3. **Everyday landmarks:** sweet shop with display trays, pharmacy, para club with benches/notices, tea stalls, rickshaw/auto stand, and an open space reserved for a seasonal puja pandal.
4. **Extend existing detail:** the code already supplies balconies, shutters, overhead wires, roof tanks, weathering and parked vehicles. Reuse these kits. Add readable Bengali/English fictional business signs: cream/red sweet-shop boards, green/white pharmacy boards, varied blue/red shutters. Avoid uniformly distressed buildings or permanent festival decoration.

**Population contract**

Use portable cohorts with seeded route slots and lightweight schedules: students arriving 06:45–07:30 and leaving 13:30–14:30; office commuters morning/evening; daytime vendors, children and elders. Resolve school days and holidays from the authoritative calendar; reconcile existing named-student schedules with the selected school shift.

Targets are **ambient people within roughly 100 m**, additional to named characters:

| Time | Target |
|---|---:|
| 05:00–06:45 | 12–20 |
| School arrival/departure | 55–75; concentrate 25–35 near school |
| Other daytime | 25–40 |
| 17:00–20:00 | 40–55 |
| 21:00–05:00 | 6–12 |

Start with caps of **48 person actors**, including named characters, plus **12 vehicle actors**. Reserve capacity for named/interacting people; use instanced block figures for up to 80 additional distant pedestrians. Near actors within 25 m receive avoidance and interaction; farther figures follow batched paths. Offscreen cohorts advance at schedule/route boundaries, never as ticking actors. Spawn/despawn behind occlusion or at credible entrances.

Movement uses plausible walking speed under the existing accelerated clock; time jumps reconcile offscreen, never visibly teleport crowds. These are profiling targets, not measured budgets.

Anonymous people offer brief greetings, directions or “heading to school”; named characters retain authored conversations. Any name, promise, relationship or journal reference crystallises persistent identity before recording it. Ambient slots remain transient, derived from world seed, district, date and slot; they consume no persistent gameplay RNG and add no crowd arrays to saves.
