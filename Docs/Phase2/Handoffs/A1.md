### Design

- **One schedule authority.** A binding repeats the bell schedule's subject for its period; a re-ordered timetable is
  refused. Arrival-crowd content never touches this.
- **Lessons tie to real cases.** Each lesson names `familySubject` and `role`; the validator requires an authored case of
  that family with exactly that subject and role. (The structure runner still picks the case from the family: a binding
  is family-wide, so a lesson's case can be any of the family's. The text therefore says "you usually sit as ...".)
- **Coverage.** Science uses all four structures (mathematics and physics model, chemistry coordinate, English explain,
  the physics lab investigates, Bengali explain). Commerce: accountancy investigates (the cash-total case), business studies
  coordinates (the stall), economics, English and Bengali explain, mathematics models. Humanities: history, English,
  Bengali explain; geography models; political science and sociology coordinate.
- **Closures.** Every holiday and break of the calendar is explained by name (validated against the loaded calendar), plus a
  generic line for each closed-day kind (weekend, holiday, break, outside year, outside term). Nothing is guessed.
- **Unavailable periods.** Twelve explanation codes, each with a reason and a recovery line: `closed_day, period_used, busy,
  not_at_bell, not_attending, history_full, invalid_state, foreground_limit, not_secure` (the `school.session.*` suffixes) and
  `missed_absent, missed_attended, after_absence` (recovery cases the view derives). Any unknown code falls back to `invalid_state`.
- **Recovery after absence.** A missed period is never replayed (no retrospective lesson is invented). The view marks it
  Missed with `missed_attended` (you were in school) or `missed_absent`, and `RecentAbsentDays` (wholly absent school days in
  the last 14 campaign days) raises the `after_absence` note.
- **Two-foreground limit.** The view counts today's foreground records including cancelled ones (as `QuerySchoolPeriod` does)
  and, at the bell, explains `foreground_limit` or `not_secure` next to the mode that is off. Attend stays available.
- **The view decides nothing.** An Open row only exists when `QuerySchoolPeriod` accepted; opening, choosing and closing remain
  the frozen commands. The panel sends only `(BindingId, Mode, GoalName)` to the bridge; skill, seed and alternatives are the
  bridge's.

## Finding worth knowing

`bellSchedule` and `calendar` are plain string fields, not registry `Ref` fields, on purpose. The registry skips all semantic checks
when any reference fails to resolve, so a hard `Ref` made `PerfectWorldAttendanceTests` ("a stream without a day is refused",
which deletes a bell schedule file) fail in the first full ctest (206 of 207). The decoder instead checks that the named record
exists and has the right type, and reports `bell schedule record is missing` / `calendar record is missing`.

## Risks and unresolved assumptions

- **Case selection is family-wide.** A lesson binds a family, so a "Physics" period can draw any of the model family's eight cases
  (including a geography or accounting one). The lesson's `familySubject`/`role` are real, but the exposed case is the runner's
  pick. Fixing this needs a subject filter in `SelectModelCase` and siblings (a frozen P2C contract); the brief texts are written
  to describe the kind of reasoning rather than a specific case.
- **Case coverage is thin.** Four cases per non-model family; each stream replays them over 34 weeks. Authoring more cases is
  the real content work behind the lesson catalogue.
- **Sports day and assessment weeks** are in the calendar, but `CalendarIsSchoolDay` stays true on them, so the contract still
  opens lessons. The view does not invent a closure for them. Decide whether they should close the timetable.
- **Early arrival crowds vs 10:00 first bell.** Untouched; the bell schedule is the only authority.
- **Production-assumption subject lists** (WBCHSE style) remain unverified, as the bell schedule notes say.
- **`History full`** (4,096 periods) is explained in words but a whole life beyond it needs the archive contract named in P2C.
- **Text is English-only and authored in one pass**; the cultural reviewer gate for festival lines (Durga Puja, Kali Puja) is
  still human work. Festival closure lines avoid dates and claims beyond the calendar record.
- **Panel is unbuilt.** Slate lambdas capture `this` through `CreateSPLambda`; the first compile may need small fixes
  (overload choices on `PWUI::MakeText`, the `FText` ternaries in `BuildModes`). The behaviour tests assume `SimulateClick`
  works on chip and mode buttons the way it does on the quest window's buttons.
