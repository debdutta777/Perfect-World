## Design

`PWA5::IStudyWorld` is a per-host shared bridge; implementations must weakly reference
UObjects. `InteractStudyDesk` asks the bridge about home/reachability before invoking
the host's open delegate. No new actor, registry, clock or stats owner is introduced.
The desk reads `SchoolAcademicRecordView`, renders stable brief IDs/text keys and
availability, and exposes existing activity/lesson offers supplied by the bridge.
`StudyOffer` reuses the accepted ActivityDef's name and duration, never an invented
study duration or skill reward. A2/A3 method panels are dispatched by the host.

The assessment panel owns only WorkId, local answer choices and focus state. Every
mutation calls `IStudyWorld::Apply(SchoolWorkCommand)`. Read/quote paths do not mutate.
Begin reserves 90 minutes; Submit charges returned WorldMinutes once. Cancel charges
spent five-minute units and resets the unsent draft. Back retains the paper; do not
resume free-roam time while the paper remains open. Survey estimates and familiarity,
unallocated units, costs and marks all come from frozen APIs. Finished-but-unsent
papers still expose Submit/Cancel. Submitted papers expose feedback only.

No decorative animation. Existing Fivefold Aperture panel/button/text builders,
scrolling body, responsive bounds, keyboard/controller Back, weak Slate delegates
and delayed focus helpers are reused. Rebuilds restore the selected desk control or
Back; disabled/disappearing assessment controls fall back to Back. Actual focus,
navigation, clipping and text-scale behavior still require an arranged UE window.

```cpp
{

        .OnOpenPaper(OpenPaper).OnClose(Close);

const auto MakePaper = [Bridge, Space, ReturnToDesk](const std::string& Id)
{
    return SNew(SPWAcademicAssessment).World(Bridge).WorkId(Id).Space(Space)
        .OnClose(ReturnToDesk);
```

At the desk's OnOpenPaper binding (the lambda's body replaces the host's panel):


an unowned UObject in it; weakly resolve the subsystem on every call. Keep panel
delegates free of strong references back to the owning host. Cancel the old host's
