# Life paths and progression

**Document status:** core design, 2026-09-20. Designed with GPT-6 (`gpt-6-astra`). Authoritative on life paths and their ladders. Answers to `VISION.md`. Everything here is proposed design; nothing is implemented. Institutional names, qualifications and numbers are fictional future-world rules inspired by real structures.

## The governing rule

**Every path needs its own definition of world supremacy, the mechanics to earn it, and consequences large enough to justify it.**

A researcher does not reach the top by becoming useful to an S-rank hunter. They reach it by discovering something that changes what all fifty nations can do.

Attributes and learned skills stay separate. Intelligence supports research; experimental design must still be learned. **Tattva affinity changes the method, never the ceiling** — an Agni clinician learns precise ablation, a Prithvi athlete excels at force transfer. Every affinity can reach every endgame.

## The ten paths

| Path | Unique verbs | What winning at world level means |
|---|---|---|
| **Hunter / expedition specialist** | Read, engage, interrupt, contain, retreat, extract | World No. 1 in a discipline, or first verified clear of an international frontier Gate. Solo technical specialist and raid captain are separate crowns |
| **Scholar / researcher / educator** | Predict, discriminate, falsify, explain, reproduce, defend | A new predictive law, independently reproduced in **five nations**. World Discovery Laureate. The educator branch wins by demonstrable learning gains adopted internationally |
| **Engineer / architect / master craftsperson** | Instrument, fabricate, join, calibrate, stress-test, redesign | The defining infrastructure of an era. World Engineering Grand Prix **plus twelve months of proven operation in five nations** — a beautiful prototype alone earns the prototype title |
| **Entrepreneur / executive / cooperative founder** | Bid, bargain, finance, insure, allocate, restructure | World-leading enterprise in a sector, having survived a full adverse cycle. Victory changes access, price or quality across nations — not merely the largest balance |
| **Athlete** | Pace, feint, position, combine, execute, recover | World No. 1, champion, record holder. Precision, endurance, team and power sports have independent prestige. Retirement is not required for the career to matter |
| **Clinician / rehabilitation specialist** | Triage, differentiate, stabilise, intervene, monitor, rehabilitate | A reproducible treatment for an otherwise disabling Gate syndrome. World Clinical Laureate, judged on sustained outcomes, not spectacular cases |
| **Public leader / diplomat / advocate** | Represent, investigate, bargain, legislate, adjudicate, ratify | Broker **and implement** a fifty-nation Gate compact. A famous speech without durable implementation does not count |
| **Logistician / emergency coordinator** | Sequence, buffer, route, prioritise, dispatch, recover | Operate the world's most resilient essential network, or coordinate an international evacuation preserving services across several countries |
| **Artist / performer / cultural director** | Compose, interpret, stage, improvise, transform, connect | A work entering the international canon, or a touring institution across fifty nations. Sales, critical recognition and cultural influence are separate success routes |
| **Ecologist / cultivator / restoration steward** | Survey, cultivate, graft, balance, restore, steward | A staple resilient to Gate contamination; a restoration method adopted across nations. The climax can be the first harvest feeding millions |

Each path supports salaried, independent and institution-leading careers. **Becoming a manager is never the compulsory final promotion.**

## The ladders

Ages describe common pacing, not deadlines. **Starting research at thirty or founding a company at fifty remains viable.** At sixteen everyone is a Class XI student; the examination creates a **portfolio of discipline credentials, not one lifelong class.**

Credential scoring is **80% discipline performance, 20% common judgment and responsibility**. Combat is absent from the common component. Mixed routes produce usable credentials in several disciplines, but financial awards share one support envelope so a mixed route cannot collect ten full scholarships.

A representative ladder — the others follow the same four-stage shape (school entry → qualified practice → national standing → international summit):

| Stage | Hunter | Scholar | Enterprise |
|---|---|---|---|
| **16–18** | F trainee; demonstrate withdrawal, rescue, team communication | Research club investigator; first defensible project *including negative findings* | Supervised school venture; keep accounts, deliver a real order, resolve a complaint |
| **18–23** | E → D licence by independent field assessment across three operation types | Undergraduate researcher or paid lab apprenticeship; doctoral by original defensible contribution | Founder or branch operator. First gate: **twelve months solvent, reliable delivery** |
| **National** | B → A licence; lead audited operations in three hazard families | Independent fellow / principal investigator; external replication required | Regional → national firm; unit economics, governance, delivery across three regions |
| **World** | **S accreditation** — international assessment of frontier capability *including failure management*. S is competence, not celebrity | International chair or institute founder; World Laureate needs transformative contribution **and** replication | Multinational founder; operate in five nations, survive stress audit. **Inherited assets do not award personal mastery** |

Three rules prevent an early mistake becoming permanent exclusion:

- **The examination is a funding and access hinge, not a lifetime permission slip.** Annual reassessment to age twenty; adult bridges and recognition of prior learning thereafter.
- **Titles, competence and current ranking are separate.** A retired champion keeps the historical title; their ranking decays through inactivity, their knowledge does not.
- **Prestigious routes save time and provide networks.** Apprenticeships, public facilities and open qualifiers reach the same summit by a harder road.

## Crossing paths

A character holds four independent records: **mastered methods, professional permissions, demonstrated achievements, relationships.** "Career" is the current organisation of those records, not an exclusive slot.

**The real cost is calendar competition, not a penalty.** With twelve useful two-hour development blocks per week after work, care, travel and rest, against a 120-block milestone:

| | Specialist | Hunter-engineer |
|---|---:|---:|
| Primary | 10 blocks | 5 hunter |
| Secondary | 2 blocks | 5 engineering |
| Integrated | — | 2, split equally |
| Weeks to milestone | **12** | **20 each** |

This is a balance model, not a visible XP bar. What the hybrid buys:

| Hybrid | Unique capability | Why it justifies the slower ladder |
|---|---|---|
| **Hunter-engineer** | **Field refit** — redesign an approved device mid-expedition after measuring the actual hazard | A pure hunter uses prepared equipment; a pure engineer never gets the live measurements |
| **Clinician-ecologist** | **Source-to-patient investigation** | Unlocks outbreaks whose crucial clues fall between institutions |
| **Entrepreneur-artist** | **Independent production** — finance and distribute while retaining creative control | Trades practice time for ownership and freedom from patron demands |
| **Diplomat-logistician** | **Executable agreement** — settlements whose schedules and reserves actually work | Resolves disputes persuasion alone cannot settle |

Interface techniques require competence in **both** prerequisites plus a joint capstone. They are specific actions, never a universal percentage bonus. No achievement double-counts toward two world crowns. **Hiring a specialist supplies their labour, not their credentials.** Sequential careers are a strong strategy — a decade of research makes later engineering distinctive, not wasted.

## Equal but different stakes

Combat has inherent drama. Other paths need equally consequential commitments without compulsory violence. Five ingredients: **something entrusted to the player, incomplete information, a closing window, an irreversible decision, and a recoverable future.**

| Path | Its dangerous expedition | What can be lost |
|---|---|---|
| **Research** | A unique sample usable for **six hours**. **Three assays** left. Two rival models predict opposite containment requirements | The sample, priority, a year of funding, facility access. Publishing too strongly forces a correction; an honest disproof preserves integrity and may still be valuable science |
| **Engineering** | Commission a city barrier before a forecast pressure peak. **Four teams, six faults, two shutdown windows** | Prototype, contract, operating authority, public confidence. Negligence causes real damage. Halting costs prestige but may demonstrate excellent judgment |
| **Enterprise** | A port closure creates a **72-hour solvency crisis**; payroll, collateral and deposits fall due at different times | Ownership, control, employees' trust. Sell shares, renegotiate, liquidate, or enter orderly insolvency. **The game never invents unlimited liability afterward** |
| **Clinical** | Several patients deteriorate with ambiguous symptoms; diagnostics and specialist time are limited | Patient function or life, team trust, future authority. **Outcomes are judged on information available at the time, not hindsight** |
| **Public leadership** | A coalition expires at midnight; one necessary clause protects vulnerable residents but loses a pivotal partner | Office, treaty, constituency trust, a once-in-a-generation reform window |
| **Ecology** | A restoration basin nears an irreversible tipping point | Seed stock, seasons of work, habitat, livelihoods, community permission. An intervention can succeed locally while exporting harm downstream |

For research, engineering, enterprise, ecology and art, **realisation is delayed** — commit now, learn part of the result later. Forecast ranges and known uncertainties keep that fair.

Major commitments state their loss envelope beforehand:

> *"Failure could consume this grant and close the laboratory for one season. Your qualification and completed publications remain."*

**Drama rhythm: ~60% routine and relationships, 30% challenging development, 10% career-defining pressure.** A calm career remains valid. Not every breakfast is a crisis.

## The World Convergence season

Ten independent world championships annually. Winning any grants the same formal status: **world champion**. A championship tests current excellence; a laureateship recognises durable contribution — so research never becomes a quiz show.

**World finals: eighty entries per discipline — one guaranteed representative from each of fifty nations, plus thirty merit qualifiers, capped at three per nation.** All fifty nations participate at world level without guaranteed medals. **Nationality grants no innate stat bonus.**

Sample events, all using the school's four interaction structures at higher stakes:

| Discipline | World final | Scoring |
|---|---|---|
| **Research** | **Unknown Law Challenge** — an unfamiliar phenomenon, limited instruments, an unseen prediction set | 40% predictive accuracy, 25% replication, 20% information per resource, 15% defensible uncertainty. **A well-proved null result can win** |
| **Engineering** | **Build Under Changing Physics** — build, then operate through two announced environmental changes | 40% function, 30% reliability, 20% efficiency, 10% repairability |
| **Enterprise** | **Continuity Market** — equal balance sheets, real rivals, binding contracts, a supply shock | 35% value delivered, 25% solvency, 20% contractual reliability, 20% resilience. **Exploiting a loophole that leaves obligations unpaid does not win** |
| **Clinical** | **Diagnostic and Recovery Championship** — simulated patients evolving across emergency, treatment and follow-up | 50% case-adjusted long-term outcome, 20% diagnostic quality, 20% resource use, 10% consent and communication |
| **Public leadership** | **Accord Assembly** — conflicting private mandates, a shared crisis, then a simulated year of implementation | 40% implementation, 30% mandate, 20% durability, 10% accountability. **No agreement is sometimes better than a destructive one** |
| **Logistics** | **Cascading City** — operate a network through linked transport, power and supply failures | 45% needs met, 25% recovery time, 20% efficiency, 10% robustness to the next disruption |
| **Ecology** | **Living Basin Challenge** — restore an unfamiliar ecosystem, then accelerated seasons and field validation | 35% function, 25% resilience, 25% sustainable provision, 15% external harm avoided |

**No real-world PhD, medical or financial expertise is required to play well.** Character mastery supplies the tools; the player chooses where to apply them.

### Three ranking layers

- **Individuals** — per-discipline, best four sanctioned results over a rolling **24 months**. All-time achievement records never decay.
- **Universities** — ten departmental rankings plus an institution index: **50% competitive performance, 30% independently verified work, 20% student development.** Limited counted results stop sheer enrolment deciding everything.
- **Nations** — each of the ten paths contributes **exactly 10%**. Within a path, 70% senior/open and 30% junior/university. **One dominant hunter cannot erase failure across nine other fields.**

Rankings buy attention and opportunity. They do not replace licences, elections, contracts or scientific evidence.

## The power problem

Why combat power cannot consume society. "It is illegal" is not sufficient.

**A. Power has independent dimensions.** Output, precision, range, duration, perception and coordination train separately. An S-rank may deliver enormous force while lacking the millimetre control for surgery. **There is no universal level multiplier to Intelligence, persuasion or craftsmanship.**

**B. Tattvas manipulate defined processes.** Restoration needs a viable biological process — it cannot infer a diagnosis. Transformation consumes feedstock — no unlimited commodities from nothing. Spatial movement needs mapped, calibrated destinations. Perception collects signals; interpretation stays learned work. **Social abilities improve clarity; they cannot manufacture consent, ownership or belief.**

**C. Preparation beats raw output in specialised environments.** Hospitals, laboratories and power stations have distributed protections. No individual defeats every safeguard, operates every system and compels every specialist simultaneously.

**D. Coordinated defence scales.** Nobody sustains maximum output indefinitely. Several prepared teams can isolate and exhaust a rogue champion — and research, engineering, logistics and clinical expertise are what make that coordination possible.

**E. Conquest does not transfer competence.** Seizing a building does not copy employee knowledge, cryptographic authority, trusted counterparties or a supply network. **Enterprise value can collapse during the capture.** Violence remains a real threat with real victims, but it is an unreliable route to owning a productive economy.

**F. Personal credit is non-transferable.** Funding a discovery earns patronage, not authorship. Owning a team does not grant its captain's ranking.

**G. Sport regulates intended capability.** Output-capped and open-power divisions coexist. The strongest hunter may enter open-power sport and excel — after learning its technique and beating specialists.

**H. Money accelerates access, not adaptation.** Wealth buys facilities, staff and protected time. It cannot bypass practice saturation, supervised competence or independent validation.

This still permits hunter tycoons and politically powerful artists — but their standing must be **earned or legitimately delegated**.

## Legibility

The player should understand a career through **a person, a playable task and a visible future** before choosing it.

Every path appears through three channels: a **local contact** (a clinician, workshop owner, coach, ward organiser); a **30–90 minute trial** using that profession's real decision structure; and a **world example** broadcast showing the same verbs at the summit. *The player repairs a school pump, then watches a world engineer diagnose an international barrier failure. The scale changes; the connection stays recognisable.*

The Aptitude Projection printout extends to:

> **Research: strong evidence.** Analysis established; experimental teamwork unobserved.
> **Engineering: plausible.** Good fault diagnosis; no structural project completed.
> **Athletics: insufficient observation.** This means unknown, not unsuitable.

**"Unobserved" and "weak demonstrated performance" must look visibly different.** Projection confidence never becomes an admission restriction.

A **career folio** phone page shows, per path: a representative week; the next two concrete gates; typical early income, costs and scheduling volatility; required permissions; current national and world leaders **including late starters**; one achievable trial and one named mentor; and the summit in both independent and institution-leading variants. Comparisons use the player's own circumstances:

> *"The laboratory route offers equipment and a stipend but adds eight weekly travel hours. The local apprenticeship preserves those hours and requires a later theory bridge."*

**Players commit to a term, contract, season or project — never an irreversible class.** The commitment screen names obligations, exit dates, sunk costs and transferable progress.
