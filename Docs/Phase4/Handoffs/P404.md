## 4. Validator Rules That Cannot Be Satisfied Simultaneously and Why

Every individual file in this pack (`p2.encounter_p404.json`, `p2.pace_p404.json`, `p2.changed_rule_p404.json`, `p2.stabilise_p404.json`, `p2.alliance_p404.json`, `p2.supply_p404.json`) satisfies all per-record schema, fairness, prefix (`p2.` and `p2.a7.`), classroom-model (`problem_case.xi_spring_mass_shm` and `problem_case.xi_dc_series_parallel`), beat-graph, and `1000/1000` score-ceiling rules in `TowerScenarioSchema()` (`TowerScenarioSchema.cpp:49-57`) and `LoadTowerScenario(record)` (`TowerScenarioContent.cpp:188-281`). However, three catalogue/contract constraints in the existing C++ validator cannot be satisfied without integrator changes to shared code or content manifest files:

1. **Catalogue 1-to-1 route uniqueness (`Source/PerfectWorldCore/Private/TowerScenarioContent.cpp:303-307` and `479-485`):**
   - `Tower.cpp:25-44` defines a closed table of 9 routes (`TowerRoutes()`), and all 9 routes are already occupied by the 9 existing Phase 2 A7 files in `Data/Content/tower_scenario/p2.*.json`.
   - `LoadTowerScenarioCatalogue` (`TowerScenarioContent.cpp:303-307`) enforces `!seenRoutes.insert(parsed.Value->RouteId).second` and emits `"duplicate scenario for route \"...\""`.
   - Consequently, when `LoadContent` / `ValidateTowerScenarioSemantics` (`TowerScenarioContent.cpp:479-485`) scans `Data/Content/tower_scenario/` with both the 9 Phase 2 A7 files and the 6 new P4-04 files present (15 records total), the second scenario on each of the 6 routes is flagged as a duplicate route binding.
2. **Standalone 6-file vs 9-route coverage (`Source/PerfectWorldCore/Private/TowerScenarioContent.cpp:317-325` and `TowerScenarioContent.h:147-150`):**
   - Order P4-04 requires authoring **exact 6** new `tower_scenario` files (one per method), whereas `TowerRoutes()` (`Tower.cpp:25-44`) has 9 routes across the 6 methods.
   - If `LoadTowerScenarioFiles` is called on the 6 P4-04 files alone with `requireFullRouteCoverage = true` (`TowerScenarioContent.cpp:317-321`), it checks all 9 routes in `TowerRoutes()` and reports `"missing tower_scenario for route"` for the 3 routes not included in a 6-file method pack (`tower.route.team_transit`, `tower.route.design`, `tower.route.mediation`). Calling `LoadTowerScenarioCatalogue(db, false)` validates the 6 scenarios and their `problem_case` references without requiring all 9 routes.
3. **`primaryMethod` on the `combat`-method scenario (`Source/PerfectWorldCore/Private/TowerScenarioContent.cpp:218-220` and `Source/PerfectWorldCore/Private/Tower.cpp:25-44`):**
   - No `TowerRouteDefinition` in `Tower.cpp:25-44` has `Method == TowerMethod::Combat`; the sole combat-capable route `tower.route.encounter` (`Tower.cpp:26`) has `Method = TowerMethod::Endurance` and `AllowsCombat = true`.
   - Because `LoadTowerScenario` (`TowerScenarioContent.cpp:220`) enforces `routeSpec->Method == s.PrimaryMethod`, setting `"primaryMethod": "combat"` on `p2.encounter_p404.json` would be rejected by `LoadTowerScenario`. Following `p2.encounter.json` (`Data/Content/tower_scenario/p2.encounter.json:8-9`), `p2.encounter_p404.json` sets `"primaryMethod": "endurance"` and `"allowsCombat": true`, and covers `TowerMethod::Combat` through its `"method": "combat"` clues and `"isCombat": true, "method": "combat"` decisions on `beat.1` and `beat.2` (satisfying `seenMethods` at `TowerScenarioContent.cpp:308-314` and `BuildOptimalCombatAttempt` at `TowerScenarioContent.cpp:269-278, 427-432`).
4. **Committed content manifest pin (`Data/Content/manifest.json`, `CMakeLists.txt:875`):**
   - Adding 6 files under `Data/Content/tower_scenario/` increases the directory inventory from 302 files / 361 records to 308 files / 367 records. Because `Data/Content/manifest.json` is a shared file outside this order's write scope, the integrator must regenerate or update `Data/Content/manifest.json` after deciding how the P4-04 pack is indexed alongside Phase 2 A7.
