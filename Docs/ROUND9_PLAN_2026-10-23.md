# Round 9 plan: the Awakening, 2026-10-23 (gpt-6-astra)

**Status:** proposed; read-only review, no files edited or verification run. Intended for `Docs/ROUND9_PLAN_2026-10-23.md`.

**Completion target:** announcement → supervised tutorial → explained, persistent Awakening → return to an ordinary day, with the examination countdown visible. This advances Round 6 upgrade #3 and M4; it does not complete M4 or substitute for the Class XI foundations.

## 1. Player-facing arc

**Announcement.** At Nabapara Vidyalaya, the familiar teacher explains the campus session, safety arrangements and examination date. Two established classmates react differently: one wants to practise together; another worries about balancing preparation with home responsibilities. Sign-in connects to the appointment, but attendance alone never counts as completing the dungeon.

**Timing.** Awakening is **23 January 2043**, ten calendar days before the national virtual-tower examination on **2 February, 06:00**. The ten-day countdown follows Awakening; it is not a delay before receiving powers.

**Tutorial.** Enter a supervised virtual floor inside the campus, with classmates and a visible teacher-controlled safe area. Three shared situations offer substantive decisions:

- **A stalled crossing:** help a frightened classmate, observe the platform cycle and explain a route, or wait together for assistance.
- **A shifting corridor:** protect someone using a movable barrier, improvise a brace, or lead the group along a longer route.
- **A shared delivery:** coordinate carriers and supplies, investigate a faulty marker, or decline confrontation and arrange a supervised handover.

Each choice requires an action with an observable consequence. Refusing to fight remains useful participation; withdrawing to safety is equally valid conduct. No powers are required. Mistakes trigger assistance and recovery, never failure, injury penalties or lost Awakening. Pausing is never interpreted as hesitation.

Awakening cannot be refused. Absence schedules a supervised make-up session before the examination; withdrawal still completes Awakening. Disclose the appointment’s time cost and reduced remaining preparation time, without reducing capability as punishment.

**Result.** Explain the five-Tattva distribution, six capability dimensions, one derived ability and starting **F-grade stage** through named evidence. Creation’s **1,000 points** establish traits; accumulated life and conduct shape the result. No class purchase, affinity purchase or paid reroll. Higher grades require assessment. “You steadied the crossing” describes evidence; “you are brave” invents a verdict.

**The ten days and day after.** Family or care staff discuss practical arrangements, meals and commitments. Classmates compare hopes without becoming grade stereotypes. Shopping, friendships, quests and household obligations continue; the calendar’s preparation break should not create school absences. Offer optional supervised practice alongside study, errands, rest and keeping an existing promise. Show displaced commitments before confirming preparation. Declining practice leaves a complete ordinary day. Examination participation remains optional, with deferment and alternative qualification routes.

## 3. Block presentation and access

Build one compact authored floor: glowing grid, labelled cuboid stations, shifting walls, soft light barriers and gentle reset pads. Use reserved Gate-kit palettes and asset IDs; no gore, lethal framing or procedural realm generation.

First person remains default; third person switches freely with identical reach and targeting. Use existing movement and contextual interaction, generous selection and no precision-aim requirement.

Provide scalable text, subtitles, keyboard/controller navigation, shape-and-text hazard cues and untimed alternatives. Reading pauses opportunity time. Reduced motion replaces moving walls with announced discrete transitions; disable shake, roll, head bob and forced camera turns. Accessibility choices never enter conduct scoring.
