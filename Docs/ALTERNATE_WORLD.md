# The alternate world: ordinary life as the game

**Document status:** core design, 2026-09-20. Authoritative on the ordinary-life simulation, whole-life play, and the world running without the player. Answers to `VISION.md`. Nothing here is implemented.

## 1. What the game is about when the supernatural is optional

The dramatic engine of an ordinary life is **the gap between the life you wanted and the life you agreed to**, measured in a currency that never refills: time.

Most games about extraordinary people ask *can you win?* This one asks *what did it cost, and was it worth it?* That question does not require a monster.

### The mechanism that makes an ordinary life land here

This world has **public rankings**. That system exists for hunters — and it is precisely what makes the clerk's life devastating.

You sat beside a boy in Class XI-B. He is now ranked fourth in India. You process insurance claims. Every year, the World Convergence is on television, and you watch it, and you go to work on Monday.

The game never editorialises about this. It simply shows you the ranking, because the ranking is public, and lets you feel whatever you feel. Some players will feel loss. Some will feel peace. **Both are correct, and the game must never tell you which one you should feel.**

That is the whole design. An ordinary life in a world that publicly measures everyone is inherently dramatic, and no other setting gives you that for free.

### Five tensions that power a mundane life

1. **Visible opportunity cost.** Every commitment displaces another. The opportunity receipt names what you gave up — only what you *knew about*, never an omniscient accusation.
2. **The world moves past you.** Classmates rank, marry, emigrate, fail, die. It happens whether you participate or not.
3. **Proximity without participation.** A Gate breaks three streets away. You are not a hunter. You process your neighbours' claims for the next four months.
4. **Dignity versus security.** The job you want pays less than the job that keeps your family housed. This choice recurs for forty years and never gets easier.
5. **Time as the antagonist.** You get one life. The game's only genuinely unforgiving rule.

### What proves this works

| Work | What it proves |
|---|---|
| **Papers, Please** | A clerk stamping documents is agonising when constraint, family and moral cost are real. Bureaucracy is drama if the stakes are someone's life and your rent |
| **Disco Elysium** | Stakes can be entirely internal. You are a wreck in one city block and it is one of the great games |
| **Unpacking** | A whole life told through objects, no dialogue, no antagonist, and it lands |
| **Cart Life** | An ordinary retail life, rendered honestly, is harrowing |
| **Ozu's films; Ishiguro's *The Remains of the Day*** | The highest-regarded work about ordinary lives is about duty, restraint and what was never said. Drama does not require escalation |

None of these needed a power fantasy. All of them needed **constraint, consequence and time**.

## 2. The unifying insight: work is school grown up

This is the architectural key to the entire vision.

`SCHOOL_AND_PROGRESSION.md` defines four interaction structures — **construct an explanation, model a system, investigate a fault, coordinate a group.** Those are not school mechanics. They are the verbs of *all* knowledge work, at every age, in every career.

| Career act | Structure |
|---|---|
| Reconcile a ledger that does not balance | Investigate a fault |
| Write a report recommending a supplier | Construct an explanation |
| Forecast next quarter's demand | Model a system |
| Get three departments to agree a deadline | Coordinate a group |
| Diagnose a patient | Investigate a fault |
| Plan a ward rota | Coordinate a group |
| Design a barrier to a tolerance | Model a system |
| Argue a case | Construct an explanation |

**This is why a twenty-career, forty-year life is buildable at all.** We are not authoring twenty career minigames. We are authoring *problem families* in four structures across many domains, and the same code runs a Class XI physics lesson, a Tuesday at an accounts desk, and a world final.

Content becomes data. Data scales. That is the whole trick.

## 3. A Tuesday as a 34-year-old accounts clerk

The hardest question in this project. Concretely, moment to moment:

| Time | What happens | Player verbs |
|---|---|---|
| **06:40** | Household negotiation. Your mother's clinic appointment is at 11:00; your sister has an exam. One of you must take her | Choose; offer a trade; redesign the obligation *(see below)* |
| **07:20** | Commute. Bus is ₹22 and 50 minutes. Metro is ₹45 and 25 minutes. You have the certification exam in six weeks and 25 minutes of reading in your bag | Spend money to buy time, or spend time to keep money |
| **08:15** | The news on the bus. A B-rank incursion in Howrah last night. Two dead. One of them is a name you recognise from school | Read or look away. Both are real choices |
| **09:00** | **Work, foreground.** The quarterly reconciliation is out by ₹2,340. Four possible causes; you have time to test two before the 14:00 submission | *Investigate a fault.* Choose tests by cost |
| **11:30** | You find it. A colleague entered a supplier invoice twice. He has been warned once already | Report it, fix it silently, or tell him privately and let him report it. **Each changes a relationship, your standing, and what he owes you** |
| **13:00** | Lunch. A colleague is organising a union petition on Gate-risk allowances for the building | Sign, decline, or ask questions. It will matter in eleven months |
| **14:00** | Submission. Then two hours of routine ledger work | *Resolve as routine.* No foreground play. You have mastered this |
| **17:30** | Commute home | Read for the certification, or rest |
| **19:00** | The evening. Four hours. Certification study, your mother, your friend who has asked three times, or nothing | **The core choice of the entire game** |
| **22:40** | The receipt. *"Studied 2h. Certification ETA 5 weeks. Declined: Ranjan (third time). Mother's appointment: covered by sister."* | Read it. Live with it |

**Foreground play that day: roughly 12 minutes.** The rest is planning, travel and resolution. That is the correct ratio — and the day contains a mystery, a moral decision, a political seed, a financial squeeze and a relationship cost.

Ranjan asking a fourth time, or not asking again, is the story.

## 4. How the awakened world changes an ordinary life

Twelve concrete mechanisms. This is what makes it an *alternate world* rather than our world with a dungeon attached.

| # | Mechanism | How the player feels it |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Property value follows Gate-risk maps.** Districts are rated. Ratings change | Your family's flat is cheap because of its rating. Moving to a safer ward costs the certification fund |
| 2 | **Insurance is the defining industry.** Everyone has Gate cover; assessors, actuaries and adjusters are a mass profession | An entire career path exists that did not exist in our world, and it is ordinary desk work about catastrophe |
| 3 | **Employers require awakening disclosure.** Some roles demand an affinity; some ban one | Your Akasha profile makes you attractive to logistics firms and ineligible for certain secure work |
| 4 | **Construction code is written around containment** | Every building has a muster point. Your workplace runs drills. You are the floor marshal because nobody else volunteered |
| 5 | **Schools stream on projected aptitude**, and everyone knows it | Your daughter's projection says "insufficient observation" and you must decide whether to pay for the activities that would change it |
| 6 | **Hunter licensing distorts the labour market.** Licensed hunters earn extraordinarily; support roles cluster around them | Your salary is measured against a boy who left school at eighteen. Public resentment is a real political force |
| 7 | **Logistics is unreliable in a way ours is not.** Corridors close, prices spike | Your shop's stock plan is a gamble every month |
| 8 | **Medicine has a whole branch for Gate syndromes** | Your mother's condition is one of them. Treatment exists, is expensive, and is rationed by a queue |
| 9 | **News is dominated by rankings and incidents** | The national mood tracks how India is performing. It affects your workplace, your family's conversation, and what politicians promise |
| 10 | **Insurance and state compensation create claim culture** | You spend four months processing your own neighbours' losses, and you know which claims are false |
| 11 | **Migration follows containment competence.** Safe nations attract people; unsafe ones lose them | Emigration is a live family argument. Your sister wants to go |
| 12 | **Memorial culture.** Dates, names, public grief, annual observances | The calendar has days that mean something. You attend, or you do not |

**Test for any addition:** if the mechanism would be unchanged in a world without Gates, it is not carrying its weight.

## 5. Life paths at full breadth

`PATHS_AND_PROGRESSION.md` defines ten paths with world-level summits. This document adds the **ordinary careers**, which need no world summit to be complete lives.

| Path | Daily verbs | What a full life looks like |
|---|---|---|
| **Corporate employee** | Reconcile, report, forecast, coordinate | Clerk → senior → team lead → manager, or a deliberate refusal to climb. Security, pension, a house, colleagues of twenty years |
| **Civil servant** | Adjudicate, process, inspect, represent | Junior officer → section → district administration. Power exercised slowly, over decades, on real lives |
| **Teacher** | Explain, diagnose, encourage, assess | Assistant → senior → head of department → principal. **Your students appear in the national rankings.** Some credit you |
| **Nurse / paramedic** | Triage, monitor, intervene, comfort | Ward → specialist → sister → matron. Constant proximity to the cost of the awakened world |
| **Shopkeeper / restaurateur** | Stock, price, serve, adapt | One stall → shop → a second location, or one beloved shop for forty years. The neighbourhood's memory |
| **Insurance assessor** | Investigate, value, decide, defend | The archetypal job of this world. You decide what a loss was worth |
| **Gate cleanup / decontamination** | Survey, clear, verify, restore | Dangerous, unglamorous, essential, unionised. The people who go in *after* |
| **Equipment technician** | Calibrate, service, certify, repair | Hunters' lives depend on your signature. You never enter a Gate |
| **Journalist** | Investigate, verify, write, publish | Local desk → national → investigative. You can end a career or expose a cover-up |
| **Lawyer** | Argue, negotiate, draft, advocate | Compensation claims, licensing disputes, liability. The legal architecture of catastrophe |
| **Farmer** | Cultivate, observe, harvest, endure | Land, season, weather, contamination. Multi-generational |
| **Homemaker / carer** | Manage, care, negotiate, sustain | **Explicitly a full path.** The household is a real system with real outcomes, and the game must never treat it as a non-choice |
| **Criminal** | Acquire, conceal, negotiate, evade | Unlicensed Gate salvage, forged certifications, protection. Real consequences, real reasons people do it |

Plus the ten from `PATHS_AND_PROGRESSION.md`. **No path requires a summit to be a complete game.** A teacher who never leaves one school has had a life.

## 6. Time: playing forty years

The model. Four granularities, player-controlled, always droppable.

| Granularity | Real time | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| **Scene day** | 20–60 min | Consequential days: first day of work, an exam, a crisis, a funeral, a decision |
| **Routine week** | 2–5 min | Plan the week, resolve it, read the receipt. Interrupts surface if something needs you |
| **Season** | 1–2 min | Four per year. Rankings update, world events fire, relationships drift, ageing applies |
| **Year skip** | seconds | Only when the player has nothing outstanding. Always offers to stop |

**Rules that keep this from becoming a spreadsheet:**

- **The game nominates scene days.** It surfaces days worth playing — never more than ~3 per routine week — and the player may always decline or add their own.
- **Never auto-resolve a first.** First day, first failure, first loss, first success in a domain always plays.
- **Interrupts have a budget.** At most one unplanned interrupt per routine week, or the plan means nothing.
- **Mastery buys compression.** Routine work you have mastered resolves without foreground play at full value, per `SCHOOL_AND_PROGRESSION.md`.

**Budget for a full life, 16 → 60:** ~176 seasons and ~2,300 weeks, of which the player plays perhaps **150–250 scene days averaging 15–25 minutes**. The rest is planned, resolved and read.

*Corrected 2026-09-23.* An earlier figure of 250–400 scene days at 20–60 minutes each came to 83–400 hours before any routine planning, which cannot fit a 60–120 hour life. The authoritative playtime budget, split by scenes, routine resolution, travel, reading and administration, is in `GAME_PLAN.md` §7 and gets reconciled against measured runs rather than assumed.

**Ageing** is calendar-driven. Physical peak ~22–32, declining slowly; cognitive and social capacity rising into the 50s; accumulated relationships and standing rising throughout. **An older character is not a worse character — they are a different one.** The endgame of a life is not a stat total; it is what exists because you were here.

## 7. The world running without you

Four tiers. This is what makes fifty nations affordable.

| Tier | Count | Fidelity | Update |
|---|---:|---|---|
| **0 — Named cast** | ~50 | Full records: schedule, career, relationships, goals, health, finances | Weekly |
| **1 — Significant** | ~500 | Career track, key stats, location, major life events | Monthly |
| **2 — Cohort** | ~50k / nation | Statistical distributions only. **No individual exists until first consequential reference** | Seasonally, in aggregate |
| **3 — Population** | millions | Aggregate numbers: employment, migration, casualties, sentiment | Annually |

*Clarified 2026-09-23.* These tiers measure **simulation fidelity**, which rises and falls. They do not measure identity. Separately, up to about 400 persistent global notables (3–8 per nation, `GLOBAL_WORLD.md` §5) hold stable identities at significant fidelity even though the player never meets most of them.

### Crystallisation

The critical trick. A cohort member does not exist as an individual until their **first consequential reference**: named in dialogue, a news story, a ranking table, a letter, a family history, or any record the player can see. At that moment the game **generates them consistent with the distribution they were drawn from**, backfills a plausible history from the world's actual event log — *this district flooded in year three, so she moved; that school closed, so she transferred* — and records a permanent identity.

*Corrected 2026-09-23.* This used to say crystallisation happens when the player *meets* someone. That is too late. Competition result tables and news name people remotely, and a champion named in yesterday's rankings must not pick up a contradictory history when the player later meets them.

**Identity is permanent; fidelity is not.** Once recorded, a person's identity and history never change. The world is therefore **consistent in retrospect** without ever having been simulated in full.

Fidelity changes automatically. Someone the player has not dealt with for two years drops to a summarised state. Their recorded identity and history stay intact, and they return to full fidelity with the same history when they matter again, because the summary is deterministic.

### Cost

Tier 0 and 1 are ~550 records updated weekly or monthly — trivial. Tier 2 is distribution maths per nation per season. Tier 3 is a few dozen numbers per nation per year. **Fifty nations of living world costs less than one Unreal particle system.**

## 8. Where story comes from when there is no hero

Story is generated from **unresolved tension**, tracked explicitly.

A **thread** is a persistent record: a debt, a promise, an unspoken grudge, an ambition, a secret, an obligation, an attraction, a rivalry. Each has participants, an escalation clock, trigger conditions and a set of authored expressions.

- Ranjan asked you out three times and you said no three times → thread: *friendship strained by repeated refusal*. It escalates. It has authored expressions at several intensities. If nothing changes, it resolves into distance — and that resolution is a scene.
- You covered for a colleague's duplicate invoice → thread: *he owes you, and knows it.* Escalates toward either loyalty or resentment depending on what you do next.
- Your sister wants to emigrate → thread: *family divergence*, clocked to her savings and the national security rating.

**Threads are the story machine.** The player generates them by living; the game escalates them on a clock; authored templates instantiate against real world state. A shopkeeper accumulates threads as reliably as a hunter — more reliably, because a shopkeeper meets the same people every day for thirty years.

**Authored spine, emergent flesh.** Fixed world disasters and the Convergence calendar provide the shared timeline everyone experiences. Threads make *your* version of it. The player's story is the intersection.

## 9. Build sequence

Each layer independently playable and satisfying. Layer 1 must already feel like this game.

| Layer | Contains | Already feels like |
|---|---|---|
| **1. One life, one year** | Clock, conditions, the four interaction structures, one school year in Kolkata, latent stats, Aptitude Projection, opportunity receipts, ~12 named NPCs, threads, save/resume | The thesis, entirely. School, time pressure, visible cost, a projection that moves |
| **2. The awakening and examination** | Tutorial dungeon, awakened profile, ten-day countdown, one tower floor with three routes, results | The complete central arc |
| **3. Adult life** | Careers, money, housing, ageing, the week/season/year compression, the ordinary-career catalogue | A whole life, not a school game |
| **4. The living nation** | Tier 0–3 NPC simulation, national rankings, competition calendar, the twelve world mechanisms, news | A country that exists without you |
| **5. The world** | Fifty nations, country level, World Convergence, international competition, geopolitical threads | The global vision |
| **6. Depth** | Remaining paths, more districts, more scene content, richer tower | The full game |

**Layer 1 is achievable and already contains the soul.** Everything after adds reach, not meaning.

## 10. What must never be compromised

1. **A player who never fights has a complete game**, not a reduced one.
2. **The game never tells you how to feel about your life.** It shows the ranking; you decide what it means.
3. **Ordinary work is played, not skipped.** The mundane is the subject.
4. **Homemaking, care and manual work are full paths** with the same design care as hunting.
5. **Opportunity receipts record only what you knew about.** Never an omniscient accusation about the optimal life.
6. **Dignity.** Poverty, failure, institutional care and ordinary jobs are depicted with respect. Nobody in this world is set dressing.
